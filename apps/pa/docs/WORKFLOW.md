# The PA workflow

The Product Advocate workflow for Contact Sales, as Fred defined it on
2026-09-30. PA emulates it and aids each step. This is the target; the
coverage map below says what is built, what is partial, and what is next.
Decisions that change a step are in `DECISIONS.md` (D58 onward).

## The stages

| Step | Stage                      | What happens                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| ---- | -------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1a   | Trigger                    | A Contact Sales form submission starts the workflow. Nothing is created or judged yet.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| 1b   | Pre-check                  | Decide whether to run the play at all, and QL, recycle, or disqualify. Support request or a recent support ticket: route to support. Educational: thank-you email and self serve. Selling to us or marketing: ignore. Fake email or message: ignore. Restricted country: disqualify. Existing open deal: route to the AE. Existing customer on the team already using Builder: point to the AE and CSM (4d). Existing customer on a different team or BU: expansion lead to the owning AE's pod (1c). Owned account: the owning PA's calendar link served on the website at form fill, then straight to 2b. Can't tell: QL it with the question flagged for the rep, never assume. Junk is disqualified with the reason logged for a weekly spot check. |
| 1c   | Route                      | Scorecard created on the record. The Scorecard Agent researches the person and account across every source, fills the qualification matrix with a source per answer, and forms a hypothesis. Owned goes to the owner, unowned round robins to the next-up PA. Scorecard and recommendation go to that rep. It re-runs on every new piece of context; every CRM write it proposes goes to the rep for approval first.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| 1d   | Immediate calendar link    | The moment 1b QLs a real company and a real person, the Email Writer drafts the immediate email with the routed rep's calendar link, answering any real question from sourced material in the prospect's language, and it sends while routing completes. A deliberate exception to human-in-the-loop, because speed is the point. A booked meeting fires Meeting Prep early.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| 2a   | Research                   | Deep Contact Research (HubSpot fields and history, LinkedIn, the public internet, prior touches, a buyer role breakdown) and Deep Account Research (company snapshot, firmographics, tech stack, relationships and deals, trigger events in the last 90 days with date and source) fill the matrix with a source per answer. Nothing found means say so. The Scorecard Agent ranks hypotheses by evidence, each with what would disprove it.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| 2b   | Decide                     | The rep is alerted in the task queue with the scorecard, hypothesis, and recommendation. Accept and sequence, decline and recycle, or research more; the rep can pressure-test the recommendation with the Scorecard Agent. With a meeting already booked, the decision is what to do with the meeting: take it, disqualify, bring in an AE, or route elsewhere. 24 hours for the QL to SAL call; no action means the recommendation executes; SLA misses are tracked per rep.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| 2c   | Act                        | Accept: the Email Writer drafts the full sequence against the live scorecard; the task calendar surfaces each follow-up with its draft; the rep approves each send from their own Gmail and LinkedIn; a reply stops the cadence; call tasks 3 per week for 3 weeks; send gates (opt-out, marketing collision, already enrolled). Decline: the Disqualify Agent recycles with the reason, the required HubSpot fields, and a re-entry trigger.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| 3    | Intro meeting              | Not the S0 Intro Call. TCQ. The agent builds the agenda and brief: ranked pain hypotheses, account-specific discovery questions with follow-ups, likely objections, a 45 second opener. Goals: their story, use case in their words, why now, what they use today, who is involved, what success looks like. Transcript to drafted answers, contacts, follow-up, and record updates for approval. Content is enterprise only; Code checks 20+ seats, enterprise features, firmographics. On a team already using Builder: stop, 4d. Decide self serve, enterprise, or not a fit, and book the next step before the call ends.                                                                                                                           |
| 4a   | Enterprise handoff         | S0 is created. The AE Handoff Agent drafts the intro email (existing owner first, else round robin), one tap to approve; the invite for the agreed time; Meeting Prep's agenda; a Slack post to the AE with the Gong call, matrix, scorecard, and the PA's link for an optional pre-sync. Tiger team when agent native.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| 4b   | Self serve                 | The Adoption Plan Agent drafts a prescriptive plan (goals, outcomes, steps, resources) from templates; the rep approves; it shares with the customer; it updates after every touch until they swipe or disqualify. Stalls, spikes, and enterprise signals alert the rep.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| 4c   | Disqualify                 | Follow-up email with a summary and resources (rep approves), the reason and required HubSpot fields, re-entry tasks or triggers, a final scorecard update.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| 4d   | Existing customer redirect | A separate, reportable exit (not lost, not recycled). Relationship state customer, existing team, with how it was detected. Reply connecting them to their AE and CSM and an internal Slack to both (rep approves), any sequence pulled. The account stays eligible for expansion if another BU surfaces.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
